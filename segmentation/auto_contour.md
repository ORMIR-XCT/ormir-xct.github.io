# Automatic contouring

The automatic contouring algorithm in the ORMIR_XCT package generates the periosteal mask of the distal and proximal bones of the wrist joint as well as a joint masking combining the distal and proximal bone masks. Either the gray value image can be imputted or a gray value image and rough periosteal masks of the distal and proximal bones. The latter is a variant for when the distal and proximal bones are too close to each other (i.e. narrow joint space width) to distinguish.

Please find details about the function for the automatic contouring based on the two approaches at the corresponding pages.
